FONTLOG for Zodiac Fonts Free
=============================

This file provides detailed information on the Zodiac Fonts Free glyph collection.

Project
-------
Zodiac Fonts Free
A curated collection of 55 astrology glyphs for modern design.


Description
-----------
Zodiac Fonts Free includes 55 high-quality astrology glyphs (Signs, Planets, Aspects, Elements and more) in Regular weight of the sans-serif family.

This is the free tier of the Zodiac Fonts collection.
The complete Pro version with all 101 glyphs across 6 font variants
(Light/Regular/Bold × Sans/Slab) is available for purchase at https://zodiacfonts.com
